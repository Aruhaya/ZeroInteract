How ZeroInteract Prevents Ticket Fraud

Traditional barcode tickets and screenshots suffer from counterfeit scalping, duplicate entries, and gate chaos. ZeroInteract utilizes deterministic cryptographic tokens linked directly to attendee wallets.

Dynamic Anti-Screenshot Pass

Every ticket is assigned a unique alphanumeric token (e.g. ZI-HACK-8812) rendered on an active canvas with animated laser verification lines to defeat static photo screenshots.

One-Time Burn on Scan

The moment an Event Manager simulates or scans a pass at the gate, the token status is immediately marked as verified in the database. Any duplicate scan attempt flashes a loud red error refusal.

Pre-Funded Wallet Integrity

Instant frictionless checkout backed by persistent client-side state. Starting with ₹800 INR, attendees can register for free workshops or purchase passes with zero payment gateway latency.

About ZeroInteract

Built for Builders, Hackers & Event Organizers Worldwide.

ZeroInteract was conceived during a high-stakes hackathon to solve the most painful bottleneck of physical and hybrid tech events: clunky registration lines, unreliable Wi-Fi verification, and scalped tickets.

Our client-first architecture leverages browser storage and deterministic cryptographic rendering to allow full offline-tolerant gate validation, instant ticket booking, and real-time manager analytics.
